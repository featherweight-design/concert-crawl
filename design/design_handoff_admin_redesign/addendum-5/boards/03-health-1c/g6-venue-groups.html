RULING
Paused reads its stored reason (crawl_pause_reason, with the resume date when set); an admin pause with no reason reads "Paused · no reason given"; a crawler auto-pause stays in FAILED. Group order, worst first: Failed, Blocked (403 or 429, danger), Paused, Robots.txt says no (warning), Quiet on purpose, OK. Hidden venues (taken down) sit last as one collapsed dashed row with a count; they are not crawled, so they never count as failing.
