RING
No shows means no genres, so the card takes the built neutral ring (NEUTRAL_GENRE_COLOR, 1.25px, no glow) and the desktop starburst is not drawn. Booked acts keep their genre ring.
UNDER THE NAME
Where genre chips sit for a booked act: one mono identity line, the same text as the search row's second line (disambiguation · area, else type · area). It is what tells two "Clip"s apart on their own pages.
ABOUT: FACTS
TYPE (Solo artist / Band / Orchestra / Choir / Other), FROM (area), ACTIVE ("2011 to now", "1998 to 2004"), LINKS (official site, Bandcamp, and the like, each ↗). A missing fact drops its row; no facts and no summary drops the whole About section.
ABOUT: WIKIPEDIA
Optional. The summary extract, whole and unedited (CC BY-SA), no clamp and no "more". Under it: From Wikipedia ↗ · CC BY-SA 4.0, both links. Never generated text.
UPCOMING, EMPTY
"No Denver dates yet." Before: why it is empty and what Follow does. After Follow, the line says you will hear. The Follow stays in the hero bar (phone) and the rail (desktop); the card carries no second Follow, as built.
