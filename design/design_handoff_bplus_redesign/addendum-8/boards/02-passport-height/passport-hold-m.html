THE RULE
0
The MRZ line ends {nights}N{venues}V from the page’s own counts: 11N8V here. The first pass showed a stale 4N4V.
1
Every leaf is 300px on a phone (the stamp page’s natural height) and 340px on desktop: the ID page and each stamp page. Nothing inside a leaf sets its height.
2
The phone stacks two leaves per view: ID + page 1, then 2 + 3, 4 + 5. That is the desktop pairing, so bookletSpreads uses a step of 2 for both layouts.
3
An odd last page gets a blank partner: four dashed empty slots and its page number, as desktop’s “next page is waiting”.
4
Pager: “SWIPE FOR PAGES 2 · 3” while more spreads follow; on the last spread “PAGES 2 · 3 · 11 NIGHTS”. The live pattern (swipeForPage / pageNights), with pages paired.
5
At 320px the leaf keeps its height; the stamps step down from 124 to 108px so two columns fit.
Why b over a: it is the cheaper build (one height constant and a step change), the ID page stays a real page instead of a strip, and the stamps never lose room to a header. Option a is drawn on its own board for comparison.
