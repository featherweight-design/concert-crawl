1
Up to 18 characters: the stamp’s base size (7.5% of the stamp).
2
19 to 24: the arc text shrinks to fit the arc, never below 5.5% of the stamp.
3
Over 24: cut at the last space before character 23 and end with “…”. Never cut mid-word unless one word is longer than 23.
4
The venue is never written on a stamp: its glyph is the venue. Long venue names don’t touch the share image. The date stays 3-letter month + day.
5
The same rule applies to the booklet stamps, which use the same component.
