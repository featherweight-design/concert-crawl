RULING · ONE RECIPE, EVERY THEME
Same geometry as Electric: a 1100×650 radial at the top left, a 900×650 radial at the bottom right, over a vertical ramp from 3% lighter than --bg to --bg (Light ramps from white). Top left is the theme's warm accent; bottom right its signal. Strength: 12 to 16% top left and 5 to 10% bottom right on dark themes; Light is 7% and 5% so it reads as a tint, not a wash. Each is a theme token pair (--glow-1, --glow-2) set in themes.ts; Electric keeps #BC77F2 as drawn. Still one glow per screen (README rule 1). The values are the swatches above.
