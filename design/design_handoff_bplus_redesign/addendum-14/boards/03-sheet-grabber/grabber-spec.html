RULING · KEEP IT, ONE GRABBER FOR EVERY SHEET THAT DRAGS
Every bottom sheet that can be dragged to dismiss shows the same grabber: 36×4, radius 2, centred, 8px from the sheet's top edge, --fg-muted at 45% (70% while dragging). It sits inside the sheet, over the gradient top edge, and adds 8px above the first content. The account sheet keeps its strip in this form; BottomSheet gains it when it is draggable; ModalShell's strip and the calendar jump grabber become this one. A sheet that can't be dragged has no grabber. Desktop has no sheets (the account menu is a popover), so no grabber there.
