ADDENDUM 15 · ARTIST PREVIEW (U3, PR #836)
Six rows the boards left open
The preview a tap on a MusicBrainz-only search row opens: the phone sheet, the palette drill-in and the desktop card. Each row is ruled below and drawn on the boards that follow. Where this differs from addendum 13, this one wins. Sample acts; the strings are the built copy.
1 · PHOTO PLATE
Panel, rounded square. Keep the build.
Panel at SLOT_RADIUS.panel (12), with the slot hairline it already wears; no second ring. 84px on the phone sheet, 96px in the palette and the desktop card (the build uses 84 everywhere). AvatarSlot is the one circle in the product, the search row's portrait. The preview is the act's own surface and stands in for the page it will get, and that page's plate is a rounded square.
2 · PITCH COPY
The built copy stands.
Copy is the copy-writer's call and #836 took it through copy-review, so Design adopts it and these boards now carry it. The board's "hear the day they book one" promised a notification a follow does not send. Design's part, the layout, is unchanged: the bold lead in the card, the pitch 9px under Follow, centred, 12.5px --fg-muted.
3 · OPEN THEIR PAGE
Under the pitch, a full-width secondary link.
Placement: 10px under the pitch, full width, only once Following and the slug are back. Style: 44px, radius 12, 1px --border-strong, no fill, 14/700 --fg, trailing 14px chevron in --fg-muted (it leaves the sheet). Hover: --fg-muted border on --surface-2. Focus: the standard ring. Build changes: radius 14 to 12, add the chevron.
4 · PALETTE BACK ROW
Keep the 44px button; restyle it and the footer.
Keep the built 44px height (over the board's 40). 0 6px padding, 8px gap, 14px chevron at stroke 2, label 13/700 --fg-muted, --fg on hover, the focus ring, 38% while a follow is in flight. The drill-in's footer changes: ESC BACK TO RESULTS · ↵ FOLLOW, then ↵ OPEN PAGE. The build keeps NAVIGATE / OPEN / CLOSE there.
5 · ERROR LINE
The preview has no load to fail. The follow error stands as built.
The preview is drawn from the search row and makes no request, so there is no "couldn't load" state to design. If search fails there is no row to tap (addendum 13, palette-error-d). The one error the preview shows is a refused Follow: FollowErrorMessage, page variant, in the pitch line's place, glyph and words, centred. --danger for 404 and 409 with Follow greyed at 38%; --warning for 429 and 503 with Follow live. No Open their page while an error shows. Confirmed as built.
6 · CLOSE BUTTON
Keep a visible Close, with the grabber.
The addendum 13 sheet and desktop card boards do draw a Close: a 36px circle top-right, next to the grabber. On main the preview renders the grabber only (#836 declined the Close). Ruling: grabber and Close on the phone sheet, Close on the desktop card, none in the palette (ESC and the back row cover it). The desktop card cannot be dragged, so without it the only exits are the scrim and Esc, and neither shows. On the phone, drag is invisible to screen-reader and switch users. Our other action sheets (report, filters, feedback) already carry one.
