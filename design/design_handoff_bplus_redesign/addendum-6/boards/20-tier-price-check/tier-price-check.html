THE RULE
•A price check belongs to one tier. Only that tier’s row changes; the headline and the other tiers stay live.
•The row takes the open-check look from the single-price card: warning left edge and tint, UPDATE PENDING, old → new?, who and when, and Submit an update.
•On the headline tier the whole card takes the band, as the single-price card does; Still this price? gives way to Submit an update, since that price is the one in question.
•Where it’s sold keeps showing the live price for that tier until an admin picks one.
•After review the row goes back to plain, with the outcome line from the single-price card (Checked by an admin…).
