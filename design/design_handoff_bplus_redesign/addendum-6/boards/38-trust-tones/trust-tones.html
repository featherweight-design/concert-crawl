Trust tones in every theme
Three new tokens: --trust-community, --trust-boxoffice and --trust-resale. They default to --signal, --warning and --danger, so five themes don’t change. Forge, Cedar and Gloss get a cool blue for Community that sits in each palette and can’t be read as Resale. Light darkens all three. Ratios are the pill’s 9.5px text on its own 8% tint over --surface-1 (AA small text needs 4.5:1).
