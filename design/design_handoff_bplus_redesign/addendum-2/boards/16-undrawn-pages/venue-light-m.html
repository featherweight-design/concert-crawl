Before you go
INSIDER TIP
It's three rooms in an old church, each with its own DJ. The main room fills first, so head upstairs if you want space to dance.
ACCESSIBILITY
Street-level entry on Lincoln. Ask staff at the door for the accessible route to the main floor.
PARKING
Paid lots along Lincoln and Broadway. Street parking thins out fast after 9.
AGE POLICY
Most shows are 18+, 21+ to drink. Have ID out before the line.
BAG CHECK
Small clear bags only. Bigger bags get turned around at the door.
WILL-CALL / ENTRY
One line for tickets and will-call. Phones scanned at the door.
GETTING THERE
Lots of rideshare drop-off on 12th. A few blocks from the Colfax buses.
Still gathering: coat check, food + drink.
