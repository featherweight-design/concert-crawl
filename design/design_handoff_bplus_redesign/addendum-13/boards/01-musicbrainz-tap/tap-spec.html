ADDENDUM 13 · MUSICBRAINZ-ONLY ROWS
Tapping an act we hold no page for
A kind-3 row has an MBID and no slug. The ruling: every tap opens something, and for kind 3 that is a preview built from the row itself. Placeholder copy and sample acts.
Nothing (Follow-only)The only rows in the list that do nothing when tapped. They read as broken, and they would need a second style to warn people off.
A full no-shows pageNeeds a slug, and the row has none until a follow creates it. A page by MBID needs a new route and the info call, and with no photo and no summary it would be the preview, made taller.
A lightweight preview ✓Everything it shows is already in the search row (name, type, area, disambiguation), so it needs no new endpoint and no request. Every row in the list now opens something.
WHAT A TAP DOES
Opens the preview: a sheet on the phone, a drill-in inside the ⌘K palette, a centred card over /search on desktop (the built ModalShell family). Plate, ARTIST, name, identity line, TYPE and FROM, "Not on a Denver bill yet", Follow. No photo, no shows, no summary.
HOW THE ROW SAYS SO
It does not need to: there is no Follow-only row. Every row opens something, so kind 3 looks and presses like the others. Its plate and second line already say "an act we know little about". No chevron on any row: the Follow sits at the end.
RIGHT AFTER FOLLOW
In the list: the row reads Following and stays where it is. A tap still opens the preview, now with Following and Open their page. In the preview: Follow turns to Following, the pitch changes, and Open their page appears under it.
KEYBOARD (PALETTE)
Enter on a kind-3 row drills in. In the preview Enter follows, then opens their page; Esc goes back to the results, a second Esc closes the palette.
ON THE NEXT QUERY
A followed kind-3 act comes back as kind 2 (it has a row now), so its tap opens its page directly, as for any of our acts.
NEEDS FROM THE API
The follow response must return the new slug. Today POST /v1/artists/follow answers { following: true } only. Without the slug, Open their page has nowhere to go and Following cannot be undone (unfollow is by slug).
