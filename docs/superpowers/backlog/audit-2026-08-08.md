## Correction to the 2026-08-04 ledger

**#82 was wrong.** That ledger recorded "only `priceDropsEnabled` pref; no pipeline." The pipeline
exists:

- `watch.pricedrop` is one of the eight locked categories, designed in
  `design/design_handoff_in_app_notifications/` (READINESS 🟢 R2)
- `apps/api/src/routes/admin/prices.ts:515` fans out on a corroborated decrease, `FOR UPDATE` locked
- `apps/api/src/routes/admin/prices.integration.test.ts:478` covers it
- `packages/push/src/watch-fanout.ts` delivers it

The real gap is that **no `watch.*` writer exists outside `apps/api/src/routes/admin/`**, so a
crawler-detected drop notifies nobody. That is the crawler-side half of #1107.
